Handles transfer speculation professionally — deflects questions without destabilising (Yahoo Sports, Nov 2025). Sole note: a 2022 scouting report flagged evolving areas (attention, leadership, error handling); this is a developmental observation on a 19-year-old, not a red flag at age 23.
