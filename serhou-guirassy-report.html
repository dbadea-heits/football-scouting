Four Corners ● ● ◐ ◐
Technical Strong›

Guirassy is a finishing technician of the highest order. In 2024-25 Bundesliga he posted 1.2 goals per 90 with 68% shot accuracy against an xG of 0.85/90 — sustained overperformance across 30 appearances. Across 2024-25 all competitions he scored 34 goals in 56 matches (one goal every 103 minutes), including a UCL quarter-final hat-trick against Barcelona. Shot selection is elite: 3.07 shots/90 in Bundesliga, generating high-value chances inside the penalty area. Ball retention under physical pressure is documented as a strength — his 83kg frame allows him to shield effectively and hold up play in tight spaces.

Limitations are honest: creative output is modest (0.3 assists/90; only 2 BL assists in 30 apps 2024-25). First touch is functional, not electric — no source cites it as a defining technical attribute. This is a pure finisher, not a technical playmaker.

Tactical Strong›

Penalty-box intelligence is his primary tactical weapon. Heatmap shows dense concentration inside the 18-yard box, capitalising on crosses and cutbacks. Off-ball movement is sophisticated: he shifts wide to drag centre-backs out of position, opening corridors for attacking midfielders (strong chemistry with Julian Brandt documented in multiple analyses). He drops deep to receive from centre-backs (primarily Anton at BVB) to link play. Bundesliga.com: "his willingness to shift wide drags defenders out of position whilst opening space for attacking partners." Niko Kovač: "fantastic awareness, stays calm in one-on-one situations."

In Dortmund's high press he is an active front-three pressing trigger — Total Football Analysis (Feb 2026) confirms he participates in the five-man press structure alongside Adeyemi and Brandt. Decision-making under pressure is rated positively across four coaching regimes. The ceiling concern is early ball recognition: he reads the game in the final phase rather than the build-up phase, which limits his contribution in possession-heavy systems expecting their #9 to engineer chances as well as finish them.

Physical Adequate›

At 187cm and 83kg, Guirassy has the textbook physical profile of a complete #9. Aerial duel win rate ~60% (above average for a centre-forward). Pressing intensity is genuine — 2.1 tackles per match reflects active defensive contribution. Stamina evidenced by sustained 56-match seasons. Speed is positional rather than sprint-dominant; he is not described as a pace threat in behind — his effectiveness derives from positioning and timing of movement, not raw acceleration. This suits 4-3-3 and 4-2-3-1 systems over counter-press high-lines requiring sprint channels.

Injury context limits this corner to Adequate. Career injuries: meniscus surgery (2016), recurring muscular + pubic bone (2017), hamstring (Oct 2023, 3 weeks), knee (Jun 2024, 3 months), ribs (Jan 2026, 2 days), muscle (Apr 2026, 5 days). No single recurring injury site since 2017; recent availability is 97% (33/34 Bundesliga matches in 2025-26). Risk classification: Medium. Fitness staff must be briefed; squad depth at #9 required.

Psychosocial Adequate›

Resilience and work ethic are his strongest psychosocial traits, forged through a decade of loans, relegations, and injury setbacks before his breakthrough at age 26. Four different coaches across three clubs (Hoeness, Sahin, Terzić, Kovač) have spoken consistently positively about his professionalism; no public coach-player conflict on record. Locker-room standing at BVB is documented as high — he was reportedly considered for the leadership council by club management.
