───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FOUR CORNERS PROFILE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TECHNICAL
Shot technique is the stand-out. In footage (Europa League vs Manchester United;
BTL highlight analysis) Óskarsson demonstrates split-second power/placement
decisions in front of goal — knows when to go near post versus when to open body
for placement, composure in 1v1 vs keeper is noted by multiple independent
analysts as "seen much more often by smaller, more agile players." Passing accuracy
is low at 52.27% (548th in La Liga among players with ≥3 apps, 2025-26), which is
a technical concern, though the figure is partly context-driven by the high
proportion of difficult passes attempted in tight spaces. Dribbling profile:
low volume, high completion rate — not a feature of his game; he relies on
positioning rather than ball-carrying to create space. Documented weakness: first
touch in transition when the pass arrives off his running line — BTL scouts noted
tendency to "get the ball stuck under his feet" on sub-optimal deliveries.
Rating: Adequate

TACTICAL
Positional intelligence inside the box is the clearest strength. UEFA's 2024-25
Europa League tactical breakdown specifically highlighted how Óskarsson "gets ahead
of his marker" to create shooting positions and uses body-swerve feints to free up
shooting angles — tactical sophistication unusual at 21. He has developed patience
in possession-dominant systems (FC Copenhagen, Real Sociedad) — balancing box
presence with occasional deep involvement without abandoning penalty-area threat.
His xA is minimal (0.12 xA for Real Sociedad, per BTL; 0 assists across 2025-26
La Liga) — he is not a creator and does not generate third-man combinations.
Heatmap zones are concentrated inside the penalty area and the central corridor
(consistent with stated role). Decision-making under press when received on the
run is strong; when forced to receive stationary, decision quality drops.
Rating: Adequate

PHYSICAL
The recurring injury pattern is the defining concern of this entire dossier.
In 2025 alone he suffered three hamstring injuries (April 13-22, May 1–June 30,
August 31–December 2025), the last of which caused him to miss 15 consecutive
matches at the start of 2025-26 before returning to training in mid-December 2025.
A left soleus muscle injury sustained on international duty (Wales vs Iceland,
19 Nov 2024) added to a 2024-25 absence. Earlier soft-tissue history: thigh
injury Oct 2022. At 21 this is not an accumulation of unrelated misfortune —
it is a recurring muscular pattern. Classification: HIGH risk per Transfermarkt
threshold (>15% matches missed, recurring muscular injuries in last 24 months).
In-play physical output is adequate: aerial duel win rate ~30% across ~5 duels/90
(not a specialist, but functional); height of 1.86m offers a physical presence.
5 yellow cards in 2025-26 La Liga (20 apps) suggests a disciplinary edge to his
physical engagement that borders on a risk.
Rating: Concern

PSYCHOSOCIAL
Iceland national team captain at 21: the strongest sourced leadership signal in
this report. Captaining a senior national side at that age — 18 caps, 9 goals —
requires both individual quality and recognized ability to organize teammates under
pressure. BTL scouting describes "supreme composure for a 20-year-old" across
multiple competitive scenarios, including one-on-ones versus keepers in high-stakes
moments. The most diagnostic behavioral data point: in 2023-24 (Copenhagen), he
scored a hat-trick in a Champions League qualifying match against Breidablik — a
team managed by his own father, Óskar Hrafn Thorvaldsson (who gave him his
professional debut at age 13). This reflects professional focus separable from
personal relationships. No contract disputes, public incidents, fines, or bans
found across behavioral search strings (coachability, leadership, social fit,
mentality, character/training). Absence of red flags is neutral, not positive —
but with a player whose public profile is this clean at 21, it is a reasonable
signal. Note: data was thinner than ideal on coachability specifically (no
coach/manager on-record quotes found about responsiveness to instruction).
Rating: Strong
