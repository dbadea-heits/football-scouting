FOUR CORNERS PROFILE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TECHNICAL                                                            Rating: Strong
Inácio's ball-playing is the definitional strength of his profile. He posts a 90.01% pass
completion rate on approximately 89 passes per game in the 2024-25 Primeira Liga campaign
(2,255 minutes), with progressive passes per 90 of 9.04 — top 99% among centre-backs
globally (FBref). His progressive carries per 90 of 1.90 rank top 97%, enabling him to
drive past pressing lines where a pass would be cut off. He scored 6 goals from open play
and set-piece across 42 appearances in all competitions in 2024-25, demonstrating delivery
and finishing quality well above CB norms. Ball retention under pressure is his most
consistently cited attribute across all scouting sources; he kills the ball away from
pressure rather than sideways, which compresses the opposing press rather than recycling it.

TACTICAL                                                             Rating: Strong
Inácio's body orientation and spatial awareness allow him to anticipate the next phase
before it develops. Total Football Analysis: "a highly mobile player who covers ground well,
shows very good awareness and has excellent body orientation that allows him to use explosive
moments in whichever direction is necessary." Positional anticipation is his defensive
method — he proactively cuts passing lanes rather than reacting to completed passes. His
ability to bypass midfield with line-breaking passes (allowing Sporting to skip press lines
entirely rather than play through them) is his highest-leverage tactical contribution. He
carries a press score of 2.6/90 and favours intelligent high-press positioning over direct
dueling, which fits any system requiring the CB to participate in the first press trigger.
His UCL sample (9 starts, avg Sofascore 7.28, peak 9.5 vs Bodø/Glimt, March 2026) confirms
the tactical quality holds at European competition level.

PHYSICAL                                                             Rating: Adequate
Inácio's speed (top speed ~34 km/h) is excellent for a CB and enables him to hold a high
line and recover behind. He is mobile, agile when tracking dribblers, and maintains output
across 42-appearance seasons — no fitness collapse signals in match logs. The primary
physical concern is aerial duel win rate: 47.7% (confirmed by multiple sources including
WhoScored, which lists aerial as a formal weakness). For a 186cm CB, this is below expected
norms and suggests he relies on positioning and timing rather than dominant aerial power.
This is not a dealbreaker in a high-press system where aerial duels are reduced by winning
second balls earlier in the sequence, but it is explicitly a risk in direct-play opponents
or set-piece-heavy leagues. Injury pattern warrants flagging: five separate muscle/ankle
injury episodes in 2024-25 (Oct, Nov, Dec 2024 × 2, Feb 2025), though 42 appearances
confirms all were brief (the longest stretch was Dec 22–Jan 6, ~2 weeks).
