scored with his nominally weaker left foot, and his shot selection —
consistently low-driven finishes from inside the 18-yard box — shows a player
who reads keeper positioning before committing to power vs placement. Dribble

consistency: shots concentrated in central positions inside the penalty area,

involvement well beyond pure finishing. His nine UCL goals in 2024/25 from just
4.8 xG — a +4.2 overperformance — is the most diagnostic tactical finding in

and pockets at the top of the box, consistent with the specified system function.
