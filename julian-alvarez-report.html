Physical Adequate›

At 1.70m, Álvarez does not compete in the aerial duel game — a structural limitation for systems delivering high balls to the striker from wide areas. His physical platform is built around acceleration, low center of gravity, balance under contact, and an explosive first step that generates separation from central defenders. Pressing intensity is his most elite physical output: noted by Scaloni (FIFA, 2026) as one of his defining traits, confirmed functional across both Guardiola's and Simeone's high-intensity press systems.

Stamina signal: 29-goal all-competitions output in 2024-25 and 20 goals in 2025-26 across 49 matches confirm he contributes deep into halves and across a full season. The absence of aerial threat is the principal physical concern; clubs whose structure relies on crosses or set-piece delivery to the primary striker must account for this gap.
