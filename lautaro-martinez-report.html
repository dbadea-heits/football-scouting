Four Corners ● ● ◐ ●
Technical Strong›

Martínez is ambidextrous: a significant share of his career goals are scored with his nominally weaker left foot, and his shot selection consistently reads keeper positioning before committing to power vs placement — low, driven finishes from inside the 18-yard box are his signature. Dribble success rate of 48% (21 from 44 attempts, 2025/26; FootyMetrics) is strong for a CF who executes short explosive bursts rather than extended carries.

Understat shot maps across eight Serie A seasons confirm shot quality consistency: shots concentrated in central positions inside the penalty area yielding a career xG/90 of 0.66 over 18,810 minutes. His close control under physical challenge and comfort executing one-touch combinations in tight spaces make him effective against both high and low defensive lines.

Tactical Strong›

In Inzaghi's 3-5-2, Martínez demonstrates advanced spatial reading: he drops between lines pre-receive, spins defenders with a planted-foot turn, and makes third-man runs that generate goal-side positioning before the ball arrives. Understat key passes/90 of 1.28 (2024/25) and 1.51 (2025/26) confirm creative involvement well beyond pure finishing.

His nine UCL goals in 2024/25 from just 4.8 xG (+4.2 outperformance) is the most diagnostic tactical signal in this dossier: elite shot-selection and positioning under knockout-football pressure. FotMob heatmap confirms peak zone inside the central penalty area and pockets at the top of the box, consistent with the specified system function of "drops short to receive." He also leads the front-line press with intelligent approach angles — Breaking the Lines places him in the top percentile of European forwards for pressures/90 across multiple seasons.

Physical Adequate›

At 174 cm he is below average for a European CF but compensates with elite explosive burst, superior aerial timing, and sustained pressing intensity. Shots/90 career average of 4.05 across 267 Serie A appearances signals consistent physical workload and willingness to occupy high-defensive zones throughout matches. His eight-season longevity at the top level, with consistently high minutes, confirms baseline physical robustness.

Psychosocial Strong›

Martínez has worked with a sports psychologist for several years — his own admission (football-italia.net, May 2026) — with visible payoff in his maturation from the "El Toro" hot-headedness of his early Inter years to measured, authoritative captaincy. Following Inter's Club World Cup exit (June 2025) he publicly demanded "top-level mentality" from teammates and stated those unwilling to compete should leave: a high-accountability leadership posture consistent with a captain who holds others to the same standard he sets for himself.

Antonio Conte, his most demanding coach: "He's a serious guy, he's committed and working. I'm glad to have him at my disposal" (Coaches' Voice). Two minor conduct items emerged in background checks: a €5,000 FIGC blasphemy fine (April 2025, plea bargain) — a cultural protocol matter in Italian football, not a character signal in a broader European context — and an agency fee dispute (NF Consulting, €8M claim) dismissed in his favor by the Milan Court of Appeal (April 2026). Neither represents a red flag.
