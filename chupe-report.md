FOUR CORNERS PROFILE

TECHNICAL
Chupe's finishing quality is the headline attribute: 25 goals in 36 Liga
apps (0.757 goals/90), including volleys, backheel goals, and headers —
a breadth of finishing technique unusual at age 21. The Scouting App's
professional video analysis (Mar 2026) confirms "excellent basic technique
and ball-level control" with an "excellent shot and acrobatic skills."
His conversion rate of ~50% on shots on target (22 from 48 SoT) is elite
by any positional standard in La Liga 2. His left foot is functional though
right is dominant. Main technical weakness: dribbling (BeSoccer dribble
rating: 35/100) and link-up play — he does not excel in tight receiving
situations requiring creativity with his back to goal.
Rating: Strong

TACTICAL
Movement off the ball is Chupe's most diagnostically impressive attribute:
he exploits offside lines relentlessly, arrives into the box ahead of
defenders, and creates separation through timing rather than pace alone.
In his La Liga 1 debut vs Atlético (2-0, Aug 19, 2026), he had two efforts
ruled out for offside — a characteristic pattern across the season that
signals aggressive positioning even in a low-block team context. He is
classified as a "Pressing Forward" by ScoutingStats.ai and actively
presses opposition ball-carriers including the goalkeeper. His tactical
limitation is limited association in build-up: he prefers direct delivery
over link-up play, and struggles to hold up play with back to goal
(identified by The Scouting App as the primary developmental priority).
Rating: Adequate

PHYSICAL
At 185cm / 72kg, Chupe is a mesomorph with above-average height for
Spanish football, a strong build, and a low centre of gravity — a
combination that gives him aerial threat without sacrificing ground-level
mobility. The Scouting App describes him as explosive with good step
frequency, above-average agility, balance, and coordination. He sustained
only one brief injury (Oct 31 – Nov 16, 2025, ~16 days) in his entire
first senior season, playing 36 of 38 Liga matches — a strong durability
signal. His pressing intensity (active high press including pressing the
goalkeeper) confirms sustained physical output through 90 minutes.
Rating: Strong
