Verdict

Yaya (2013/14) — The Complete B2B Playmaker
20 league goals from midfield is generational. Deep-lying passing (90.76%, 76/game) + striker finishing + physical dominance (10 set piece goals). No current midfielder combines all three facets.

Bellingham (2025/26) — The Modern Advanced B2B
Closest active player to Yaya's profile. 100 progressive carries (#58 La Liga), 156 dangerous attacks initiated. But 0.47 G+A/90 vs Yaya's 0.83 shows the gap. Creates through dribbling and line-breaking passes rather than set-piece dominance.

Bernal (2025/26) — The Young B2B Carrier
My earlier read was wrong — Bernal is not a static 6. At 191cm, left-footed, with 0.80 shots/90, 78.6% dribble success, and 55.3% duel win, he's carrying the ball forward, arriving in the box, and shooting from distance. The defensive numbers (6.75 contributions/90, 3.0 tackles/90) just show he does both phases — that's the B2B job. At 19, with 787 senior minutes, his 0.34 G+A/90 is respectable for a developing 8, and his 92% passing accuracy is elite for any role. The raw materials (size, left foot, duel strength, ball-carrying) are genuinely exciting.

Bottom line: Bernal is a proper B2B prospect — more of a ball-carrier/shooter than Yaya (who was a playmaker/finisher) and more defensively active than Bellingham (who's more about progressive carrying and chance creation). At age 19, he's ahead of where Yaya was at the same stage. If his offensive output grows from 0.34 to 0.50-0.60 G+A/90 by age 22-23, he's a genuine heir. The ceiling is real.
