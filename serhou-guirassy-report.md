───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FOUR CORNERS PROFILE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TECHNICAL
Rating: Strong

Guirassy is a finishing technician of the highest order. In 2024–25 Bundesliga he
posted 1.2 goals per 90 (68% shot accuracy per Breaking The Lines) against an xG of
0.85/90 — sustained overperformance across 30 appearances. Across 2024–25 (all
competitions) he scored 34 goals in 56 matches at one goal every 103 minutes, including
a hat-trick in the UCL quarter-final against Barcelona. His shot selection is elite:
Understat records 3.07 shots/90 in 2024–25 Bundesliga, generating high-value chances
inside the penalty area. Ball retention under physical pressure is documented as a
strength (82–83kg frame; described by coaches as able to shield and hold effectively).
First touch is functional at Bundesliga level — adequate rather than elite (no tactical
source cites it as a standout attribute; his creation output is limited: 0.3 assists/90,
with only 2 assists in 30 BL apps 2024-25).
Rating: Strong

TACTICAL
Rating: Strong

Penalty-box intelligence is his primary tactical weapon: heatmap shows dense
concentration inside the 18-yard box, capitalising on crosses and cutbacks rather than
creating his own opportunities. His off-ball movement is sophisticated: he shifts wide
to drag centre-backs out of position, opening space for attacking midfielders (Brandt
chemistry documented), and drops deep to receive from centre-backs (particularly Anton
at BVB) to link play. In the Stuttgart two-striker system he functioned as a target man
drifting left; at Dortmund in a lone-striker role he anchors the press from the front.
Total Football Analysis (Feb 2026): Guirassy participates in Dortmund's high five-man
press, working in coordination with the front three. Bundesliga.com: "his willingness
to shift out wide to receive drags defenders out of position, opening space for partners."
Decision-making under pressure is strong — Kovač specifically cites calmness in 1v1
situations. The tactical ceiling concern is limited creativity: in two full BVB seasons,
assists remain modest (6 total across 56 games 2024-25, predominantly from UCL). He
reads the game late rather than early when possession-building.
Rating: Strong

PHYSICAL
Rating: Adequate

At 187cm and 83kg, Guirassy has the physical profile of a complete #9. Aerial duel win
rate is approximately 60% (Breaking The Lines), above average for a centre-forward.
Pressing intensity is genuinely high — 2.1 tackles per match suggests active defensive
contribution, not a decorative front presence. Stamina is evidenced by sustained output
across 56-match seasons. Speed: not described as a pacey striker; his strength is
positional intelligence rather than top-end acceleration, which is typical of the classic
#9 archetype. This suits 4-3-3 or 4-2-3-1 systems more than counter-press high-line
setups demanding a sprint threat in behind. Injury concern is the limiting factor in
this corner: early career meniscus (2016), muscular recurring (2017), pubic bone (2017),
hamstring (Oct 2023, 3 weeks), knee (Jun 2024, 3 months), ribs (Jan 2026, 2 days),
muscular (Apr 2026, 5 days). Pattern suggests no recurring single injury site since 2017,
and 2025-26 availability (33 apps) is the best of his BVB tenure. Risk is Medium, not
dealbreaker-level, but must be factored into squad depth planning.
Rating: Adequate

PSYCHOSOCIAL
Rating: Adequate

Two strong positive signals from sourced interviews. (1) Mentality under adversity:
"I don't need love to perform. When you're not doing well, you take refuge in work...
I push myself, so I add another layer of work. I forged this mentality thanks to what
I've been through." (Foot Africa, Mar 2025.) This is not PR language — it is grounded
in a decade of loans, relegations, and injury setbacks before his breakthrough at 26.
(2) Cultural identity and leadership beyond the club: Guirassy visited Guinea's national
training camp days before CHAN 2026 to deliver a motivational address as a humanitarian
gesture, praised by teammates and federation officials. Shortlisted for 2025 CAF Men's
Player of the Year. Locker-room standing at BVB reportedly strong (management considered
adding him to leadership council). One documented red flag: September 2025, Guirassy
argued visibly with Bensebaini over penalty-taking duties against Juventus (UCL, 4–4);
management held a meeting; no disciplinary action taken. Kovač: "there are no
repercussions." Context: Guirassy had drawn the penalty himself after having his shot
blocked on the line, triggering a competitive instinct common in elite finishers. More
competitiveness than social breakdown, but worth monitoring with a new club for clear
penalty hierarchy protocols. Coachability: four different coaches in three clubs (Hoeness,
Sahin, Terzić, Kovač) have consistently spoken positively about his professionalism and
work ethic; no coach-public conflict in record.
Rating: Adequate
