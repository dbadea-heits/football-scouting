Four Corners ● ◐ ● ◐
Technical Strong›

Finishing breadth is the headline: 25 goals including volleys, backheel goals, and headers at age 21 — an acrobatic range unusual at this developmental stage. The Scouting App (Mar 2026) confirms "excellent basic technique and ball-level control" with "excellent shot and acrobatic skills." His conversion rate of ~50% on shots on target (22 from 48 SoT) is elite by Liga 2 striker standards. Right foot dominant; left foot functional. Works both feet with reasonable comfort.

Primary technical weakness: dribbling (BeSoccer: 35/100) and link-up play — does not excel in tight-space receiving situations or back-to-goal phases. First touch under pressure is rated adequate-to-good by The Scouting App — ball killed into space rather than heavy touch. No evidence of high pass-completion under pressure (not his design role).

Tactical Adequate›

Off-ball movement is diagnostically impressive: Chupe exploits offside lines constantly, arrives in the box ahead of defenders, and creates separation through timing rather than pace. His two efforts ruled offside in the La Liga 1 debut vs Atlético (Aug 19, 2026) are characteristic — aggressive late timing on runs. ScoutingStats.ai classifies him as a "Pressing Forward," and The Scouting App confirms he actively presses even the goalkeeper, showing tactical discipline in the pressing phase even in a deep-block system.

Key tactical limitation: not an associative striker. Prefers direct delivery over link-up play; struggles holding up with back to goal. "During the possession phase, Chupe isn't a very associative striker: he must improve in link-up plays between midfield and attack" (The Scouting App, Mar 2026). Decision-making in direct situations is strong; in build-up and tight-space combinations, it is still developing. This is the highest-priority area for coaching development.

Physical Strong›

185cm / 72kg mesomorph with strong build, well-defined musculature, and a low centre of gravity — an unusual combination of aerial presence and ground-level agility. The Scouting App describes him as explosive with good step frequency, above-average agility, balance, and coordination. Sustained pressing work through 90 minutes without visible fatigue signals across a 36-app season is a durability indicator.

Injury risk: Low. Only one injury recorded across his entire first professional season — Oct 31 to Nov 16, 2025 (~16 days), with 36 of 38 Liga matches played. No recurring muscular injury pattern. Physical profile is ideal for a modern high-press #9 at La Liga standard. The step up in pace and press intensity in La Liga 1 remains an open question — one game against Atlético in a low-block system is insufficient data.
