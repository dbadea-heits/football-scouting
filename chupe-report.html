Technical Strong›

Finishing breadth is the headline attribute: 25 goals at 21 including volleys, backheel goals, and headers — a range of technique unusual at this developmental stage. The Scouting App (Mar 2026) confirms "excellent basic technique and ball-level control" with "excellent shot and acrobatic skills." His conversion rate of ~50% on shots on target (22 from 48 SoT) is elite by any La Liga 2 striker standard. Right foot dominant; left foot functional. Works both feet with reasonable comfort.

Primary technical weakness: dribbling (BeSoccer: 35/100) and link-up play — he does not excel in tight-space receiving situations or back-to-goal phases. First touch under pressure is adequate-to-good (The Scouting App describes "excellent basic technique and ball-level control") — ball killed into space rather than trapped underfoot; no heavy-touch pattern reported across the season.

Tactical Adequate›

Off-ball movement is diagnostically impressive: Chupe exploits offside lines relentlessly, arrives in the box ahead of defenders, and creates separation through timing rather than pace alone. Two efforts ruled offside in the La Liga 1 debut vs Atlético (Aug 19, 2026) are characteristic — aggressive late timing on runs is his consistent pattern. ScoutingStats.ai classifies him as a "Pressing Forward," and The Scouting App confirms he actively presses even the goalkeeper, showing pressing discipline in the non-possession phase even within a deep-block system.

Key tactical limitation: he is not an associative striker. "During the possession phase, Chupe isn't a very associative striker: he must improve in link-up plays between midfield and attack" (The Scouting App, Mar 2026). Prefers direct delivery over combination play; struggles holding up with his back to goal. Decision-making in direct situations is strong; in build-up and tight-space combinations, it is still developing. This is the highest-priority coaching development area.

His aerial ability allows headed goals; low centre of gravity aids balance in tight spaces despite the height.
