Four Corners
Technical Strong

Top-decile goal efficiency and chance creation against CF positional cohort (ScoutingStats.ai). Over three Bundesliga seasons his shot volume has led or ranked top-3 in the league — 4.6 shots/90 (#1 Bundesliga, 2023–24); 2.2 SoT/90 (#1 big-five leagues); 4.95 shots/90 (#1 Bundesliga, 2025–26). Both-footed, capable of finishing across his body from tight angles.

Weakness: Lower-third percentile for aerial duels across all sources. Consistent with 179cm stocky frame. Not a dealbreaker for a link-play CF — a dealbreaker if the system relies on long-ball aerial service.

Tactical Strong

Leads Stuttgart in attacking sequence involvements per 90 (7.8 — Opta), outperforming every teammate. In Stuttgart's 1-4-2-4 build-up shape, drops between lines to force centre-back decisions, then exploits the space created. Adapts movement to partner's positioning (Guirassy partnership was the textbook example).

xA/90 of 0.78 (7th Bundesliga, 2023–24) confirms he creates as well as converts. Progressive carrying and passing in top percentile. Heatmap data shows significantly wider pitch coverage at Stuttgart vs. Brighton — demonstrable tactical adaptability.

Physical Adequate ⚠

Documented sprint improvement: 19.6 → 26.9 sprints/90 (+37%) from PL to Bundesliga, reflecting both role adaptation and conditioning work. Stuttgart is top-3 Bundesliga for defensive pressing actions — he meets that threshold.

Injury flag — HIGH RISK. Recurring soft tissue pattern across three seasons: Knee (Aug–Sep 2023, 6 wks), Thigh+Muscle (Feb 2024, 3 wks), Muscle (Oct 2024, 1 wk), Thigh (Nov 2024–Jan 2025, 9 wks), Knee (Aug–Oct 2025, 6 wks). Estimated 20%+ matches missed in 2024–25. Recurring muscular injuries in multiple regions = HIGH risk classification. Full medical due-diligence required before any commitment.

Psychosocial Adequate ⚠

Positives: Dressing-room leader at Stuttgart — praised by Hoeneß ("must be mentioned first and foremost") and teammates. Deleted personal Instagram for mental clarity. Overcame release from Werder Bremen at ~16, years in Germany's 4th tier, only became professional at 23 — genuine resilience, not narrative. Committed to Stuttgart long-term.

Yellow flag: Public conflict with Germany coach Nagelsmann (March 2026) — Undav challenged his substitute role publicly; Nagelsmann responded dismissively before apologising after widespread criticism. Post-World Cup, Nagelsmann singled him out for a costly error, rejected by fans and teammates. Context: environment-specific (national team politics). No parallel incidents at club level. Probe in interview for response to authority conflict.
