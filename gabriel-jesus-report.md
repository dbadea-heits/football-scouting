PHILOSOPHY FIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Target role: Rotation #9 / wide forward for a possession-and-press top-European club. The
acquiring side needs a squad forward who presses from the front, maintains possession in
tight spaces, and contributes 10–15 goal involvements per season as a rotation option
behind or alongside a primary striker. He must be deployable centrally (false 9) or
inverted from the right. Age ceiling: 32 (2–3 year contract horizon). Market value ceiling:
€20M. Hard constraints: (1) medical clearance post-second ACL; (2) squad-player wage
structure at new club; (3) no primary-striker role expectation.

ROLE FIT: Jesus is a natural fit as a squad rotation striker — his pressing, progressive
carry ability, and off-ball movement are precisely the profile described. The false-9
adaptability is proven across City and Arsenal tenures.

SYSTEM FUNCTION: Fit. His career at both City and Arsenal demonstrates ability to
integrate in possession-based high-press systems. Tactical corner is Strong.

AGE/VALUE: Fit. At 29 and €10M, this is a squad-rotation price, not a starter investment.

INJURY CONSTRAINT: MISFIT. Two ACLs (right knee 2022, left knee 2025) plus recurring
groin/thigh issues constitute HIGH injury risk. The hard constraint of medical clearance
is not yet confirmed at report date.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FOUR CORNERS PROFILE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TECHNICAL
Jesus is a statistically elite progressive ball-carrier and passer for a forward — 5.68
progressive carries per 90 (90th percentile, forwards cohort, FBref) and 2.68 progressive
passes per 90 (90th percentile) when at his peak at Manchester City. These figures explain
ScoutingStats.ai's verdict that progressive passing and carrying are his standout
attributes, confirmed by 3.15 deep completions per 90 (92nd percentile). His first touch
in open space is clean and directional; under heavy pressure in the penalty box, however,
he takes extra touches that cost him conversion angles — this is the mechanical explanation
for his career-long xG underperformance (79 goals vs 106.87 xG across 243 Premier League
apps; Understat). Dribble success rate of 46.2% in 2025-26 (ESPN) is adequate for a
forward of his profile.
Rating: Adequate

TACTICAL
Game intelligence is Jesus's highest-value attribute. He checks his shoulder habitually
before receiving, arrives in pockets early to create separation, and consistently presents
half-open body position to facilitate progressive distribution. His key passes per 90 of
1.68 (2023-24, Understat) confirm he is a creative link player as well as a finisher. At
Arsenal his heatmap shows activity across the entire front line — he is not a static
penalty-box forward. His pressing is tactically disciplined: he runs the correct angles to
force opponents towards the sideline rather than ball-watching, and ranked top 20 among
Arsenal forwards for pressures per 90. ScoutingStats.ai identifies "instinctive
positioning and knack for finding space" as a core trait.
Rating: Strong

PHYSICAL
This is the principal risk corner. Jesus has suffered TWO anterior cruciate ligament
injuries (right knee meniscus + collateral damage at the 2022 World Cup; left knee ACL
January 12, 2025 vs Manchester United) plus a groin injury (August 2024). He missed 38
matches following the January 2025 ACL — returning December 10, 2025 — and started only
9 of 27 Arsenal appearances across all competitions in 2025-26 (1,323 minutes; FCtables).
He played 421 PL minutes in 14 apps, averaging under 31 minutes per appearance. Pressing
intensity remains visible in footage post-return, but stamina output over 90 minutes is
unconfirmed. Bilateral knee damage in a 29-year-old striker is a career-trajectory
concern, not a momentary setback.
Rating: Concern

PSYCHOSOCIAL
Two sourced signals of high reliability. (1) Manager Arteta on signing (Arsenal.com, Jul
2022): "Start with his character, the person, the smile and what he represents as a human
being. Then he's an incredible professional, with his work ethic and enthusiasm — you will
see it the first day he puts on his boots." (2) Arsenal published Jesus's first-person ACL
rehabilitation diary ("Jesus' Journey," Arsenal.com), demonstrating self-directed
engagement and transparency under adversity, not deflection. No red flags identified
across behavioral search strings: no contract disputes, no public incidents, no fines or
bans. The sole nuance is that Jesus's stated wish to remain at Arsenal (ESPN, Jan 2026)
appears driven partly by being the club's third-highest earner rather than pure sporting
ambition — a motivation-alignment risk at the new club if minutes are limited. His removal
from the Brazil 2026 World Cup squad for lack of playing time adds a psychosocial burden
not yet fully visible in public sources.
Rating: Adequate

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DORMANT POTENTIAL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Type: System Gap

Evidence: At Arsenal in 2025-26, Jesus started only 9 of 27 appearances across all
competitions and logged 421 Premier League minutes — squad rotation with no back-to-back
starts in a system that prioritised Kai Havertz and Viktor Gyökeres (signed summer 2025)
as primary strikers. Despite this, his xG90 in 2025-26 was 0.91 (Understat, 14 PL apps)
vs his career PL average of 0.69/90 — shot generation was intact when given opportunities.
In his first full season at Arsenal (2022-23), starting consistently for the first time in
his career, he posted 11G + 6A in 26 apps. The system gap is real: as Arsenal's third
striker and fourth-choice forward post-Gyökeres, his PL output (3G, 0A in 421 min in
2025-26) understates his chance-generation ability. At Barcelona as a dedicated rotation
option — likely 20–25 La Liga appearances — the counting output should recover to the
8–12 goal involvement bracket.

Age gap check: Ruled out — Jesus is 29, well above the U23 threshold.

Metric gap check: xG90 exceeds goals/90 by >0.15 in every documented Premier League
season (career gap = 27.87 goals over 243 apps). This IS technically a metric gap by
definition. However, the gap is career-long and structural — it reflects a permanent
finishing inefficiency (extra touch in the box, shot selection under pressure), NOT
unlocked potential. System change does not resolve finishing mechanics. Classified as a
permanent characteristic to be priced in, not a dormant upside signal.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VERDICT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tier:       Watch

Rationale:  Jesus's Tactical corner is genuinely Strong — the pressing, off-ball
movement, and progressive action metrics are validated across multiple seasons and sources.
The system-gap classification is defensible: Arsenal's rotation role structurally
suppressed counting stats, and a more defined squad position at Barcelona should restore
10–12 goal involvements per season. However, two ACL tears in a 29-year-old striker
constitute an irreducible Physical concern that cannot be resolved from data or archived
footage. The Adequate Technical rating reflects a real finishing deficit (career 79G vs
107 xG) that system change will not cure. At €10M the fee reflects the risk profile
correctly, but the scouting verdict must sit at Watch until independent medical clearance
is confirmed.

Next step:  Commission independent two-knee MRI review and return-to-play assessment
before completing transfer paperwork. If medical clears both knees and confirms full
ACL graft integrity, elevate to Shortlist and arrange one full live observation (La Liga
Week 3–5, September 2026) to assess 90-minute pressing output and physical recovery
completeness.
